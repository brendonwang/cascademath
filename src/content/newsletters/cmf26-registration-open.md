Hi everyone! Our team is excited to announce that registration is now open for Cascade MathFest! Cascade MathFest is a day of fun problem solving and collaboration for middle school students. It will include both individual and team contests, as well as a one-of-a-kind math festival. The festival will feature a large variety of events for students to choose from, ranging from an Estimathon to Olympiad Geoguesser. This event is a great way for students to improve their math skills, with high quality problems written by MOPpers and USA(J)MO awardees, while having fun in the process!

The event is from 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM on Saturday, September 19th, 2026, and there is a $10 registration fee per competitor. Lunch will be included, and the top winners in events and the contest will receive a variety of prizes ranging from trophies to Cascade Math themed blind boxes.

Register now in teams of up to 4: [cascademath.org/cmfregistration26](http://cascademath.org/cmfregistration26)

If you have any questions, feel free to email us at [cascade.math.cm@gmail.com](mailto:cascade.math.cm@gmail.com), or check out our website: [https://cascademath.org/](https://cascademath.org/)

Thank you, Cascade Math Team
